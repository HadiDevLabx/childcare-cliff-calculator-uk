# Childcare Cliff Calculator — One Pound Over £100,000

> One pound over £100,000 adjusted net income and you lose Tax-Free Childcare **and** the funded hours outright. Not tapered. Gone.

Reference notes for the UK tax year **2026/27**, computed from HMRC's
published rates rather than copied from another site.

## How it works

Unlike almost everything else in the UK tax system, this is a genuine cliff edge rather than a taper.

What you lose at £100,000 + £1:

- Tax-Free Childcare, worth up to £2,000 per child a year
- The funded hours for eligible working parents

For a family with two children in nursery the combined loss can exceed £20,000 of value, which means a pay rise from £99,000 to £101,000 can leave you **thousands worse off**.

The fix is a pension contribution large enough to bring adjusted net income back under £100,000. In that band the contribution costs about 40p in the pound of take-home.

## What this does not cover

Both parents are tested individually for the cliff, but each must be under the limit. Two parents on £99,000 keep everything; one on £101,000 loses it for the household.

## Source and tax year

Figures are for the 2026/27 tax year, which runs 6 April 2026
to 5 April 2027, and were checked against
[gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027) on 28 August 2026.

Rates change every April, and often at a Budget. Check the tax year a figure
belongs to before relying on it.
